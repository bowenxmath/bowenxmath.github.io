Research

Research Overview

My research lies primarily in applied probability, stochastic control, queueing theory, and stochastic networks, with applications in operations research and mathematical finance.

I am particularly interested in heavy-traffic approximations, matching systems, stochastic control, limit order books, reinforcement learning, and related problems involving stochastic systems.

Selected Research Projects

Queueing Systems and Heavy Traffic

My research in queueing theory focuses on stochastic systems in heavy-traffic regimes, including matching queues, queues with abandonment, admission control, and diffusion approximations.

Stochastic Control and Optimization

I study stochastic control problems arising in queueing systems, operations research, and other stochastic dynamical systems. My work uses techniques from diffusion control, Hamilton–Jacobi–Bellman equations, and asymptotic analysis.

Limit Order Books and Market Making

I am interested in stochastic models for limit order books and market-making problems, including the development of tractable stochastic models and associated control and optimization problems.

Data-Driven Stochastic Modeling

I am also interested in combining stochastic-process models with data-driven methods, including applications of machine learning and reinforcement learning to stochastic systems.
