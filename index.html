Bowen Xie is a tenure-track Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics, College of Engineering and Polymer Science at The University of Akron (UA).

Before joining UA, he spent two years as a Postdoctoral Lecturer in the Department of Statistics and Data Science at Washington University in St. Louis (WashU). During his time at WashU, he worked primarily on market-making problems, particularly the dynamics of the limit order book (LOB), with his mentor Dr. José E. Figueroa-López .

He earned his Ph.D. in Mathematics from Iowa State University (ISU) in 2022, where he was advised by Dr. Ananda Weerasinghe and conducted research in queueing theory and stochastic control. He also holds a Master of Science degree in Applied Mathematics from Iowa State University.
